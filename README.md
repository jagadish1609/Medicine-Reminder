# Smart Medicine Reminder Box (SMR)

## Overview  
Taking medication at the wrong time or missing doses can cause serious health problems, including delayed recovery or even death. The Smart Medicine Reminder Box (SMR) is designed to help patients, especially older adults, take their medications correctly and on schedule.

## Features  
- **LCD Display & Buzzer**: Provides visual and sound alerts at set times.  
- **Motor Driver Circuit**: Controls pill dispensing within the box.  
- **GSM Module**: Sends SMS reminders to patients and caregivers.  
- **Missed Dose Alerts**: Notifies caregivers immediately if a dose is skipped.  
- **Smartphone App**: Allows users or caregivers to manage schedules, check adherence, and view usage data.  

## Benefits  
- Ensures regular and timely medication intake.  
- Reduces risks of overdosing or underdosing.  
- Provides peace of mind to patients and caregivers.  
- Combines healthcare needs with technology for better medication adherence.  

## Goal  
The SMR delivers an effective, easy-to-use solution that improves patient health outcomes by blending smart technology with everyday healtcare routines.
